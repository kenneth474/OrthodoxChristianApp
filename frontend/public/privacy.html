Privacy policy

Orthodox Daily · Last updated October 24, 2026

Orthodox Daily is a free app with the day's readings, saints, fasting, the Bible and prayers for Antiochian Orthodox Christians. You can use all of it without an account. This page explains what the app keeps about you if you create one, and what it does with it.

What the app keeps

  • Your account: your name and email address, and your phone number if you choose to add it.
  • Your password, if you use one, only in a scrambled form (a "hash") that can check a password but can't be turned back into it. Nobody, including the app's administrator, can see your password.
  • Your profile, if you fill it in: a photo, your patron saint and their feast day, and your home parish.
  • Your settings: the style, text size and light or dark look you choose.
  • If you sign in with Google: Google tells the app your name, your email address (which Google has confirmed) and an ID number for your Google account. The app never sees your Google password or anything else in your Google account.

Cookies and your device

The app uses two cookies, only to keep you signed in: one for your current visit, and one that keeps you signed in until you sign out (it lasts up to 400 days and is renewed when you use the app). There are no advertising or tracking cookies, and no analytics. The app also remembers a few choices in your browser, such as your style and your reading voice and speed. Reading aloud uses the voices already on your phone or computer, so the text being read isn't sent anywhere.

How it's used

Only to run the app: to sign you in, show your profile, keep your settings on every device, and wish you a happy name day. Your information is never sold, rented or shared, and never used for advertising. The app doesn't send emails or text messages.

Who can see it

You can see and change your own account in the Profile tab. The app's administrator can see the list of accounts (name, email, phone number, how each person signs in, and when they joined) in order to look after the app.

Where it's stored

On Render, a hosting company in the United States. Everything between your device and the app travels encrypted (https). The calendar comes from orthocal.info; when the app asks it for a day's readings, nothing about you is sent. If you sign in with Google, Google's own privacy policy applies to your Google account.

Deleting or correcting your information

You can change or remove your phone number, photo and profile yourself in the Profile tab. To delete your account and everything in it, email kennethkharelyt@gmail.com; your account stays until you ask.

Children

Children under 13 shouldn't create an account of their own. If one has, email us and we'll delete it.

Changes

If this policy changes, this page will say so, with a new date at the top.
